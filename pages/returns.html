Returns and Refunds

General guidance for product returns and refund requests.

Seller policy controls

Return windows, eligibility, restocking charges, and refund timing are determined by the seller and the order channel. Review the policy shown before completing a purchase.

Starting a return

Use the seller’s order support process and include the order number, product name, reason for return, and photographs where damage or incorrect fulfillment is involved.

Condition and packaging

Unless the seller states otherwise, keep the item, accessories, and packaging in the condition required by the applicable return policy.
