The Story of the Pen

From the first inked marks to modern precision instruments

Ancient Rome
The Stylus Era

Romans used thin metal rods called stylus to leave marks on wax tablets. This was the earliest form of the modern pen.

1560
First Wooden Pen

Italian couple Simonio and Lyndiana Bernacotti invented the first wooden pen using a hollowed-out stick of juniper wood.

1662
Mass Production Begins

Nuremberg, Germany became the birthplace of the first mass-produced pens, led by companies like Faber-Castell.

1795
Modern Pen Invented

Nicholas-Jacques Conte invented the modern pen by mixing ink with clay, creating different hardness levels.

1858
Eraser Addition

Hymen Lipman patented the first pen with an attached eraser, revolutionizing the writing experience.

Today
Innovation Continues

Modern pens incorporate advanced materials, ergonomic designs, and specialized formulations for various applications.

About MagazineHoldersCraft

For over 25 years, MagazineHoldersCraft has been at the forefront of writing instrument innovation, combining traditional craftsmanship with cutting-edge technology.

Founded in 1998 by master craftsman Robert Chen, our company began as a small workshop dedicated to creating the perfect pen. Today, we're proud to be a leading manufacturer of premium writing instruments, serving artists, students, and professionals worldwide.

Our commitment to quality, sustainability, and innovation drives everything we do. From sourcing the finest materials to implementing eco-friendly manufacturing processes, we ensure that every pen meets our exacting standards.
